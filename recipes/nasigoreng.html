nasi goreng

deskripsi

Nasi goreng is a popular Indonesian fried rice dish that is often served with a variety of toppings, such as fried eggs, chicken, shrimp, and vegetables. It is typically flavored with sweet soy sauce, garlic, shallots, and chili peppers, giving it a unique and delicious taste.

bahan-bahan

  • 2 cups cooked rice (preferably day-old rice)
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 1-2 red chili peppers, sliced (adjust to taste)
  • 1/2 cup cooked chicken, shrimp, or tofu (optional)
  • 2 tablespoons sweet soy sauce (kecap manis)
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fried eggs and sliced cucumbers for garnish (optional)

cara membuat

  1. Heat vegetable oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. Add garlic and onion, and sauté until fragrant and translucent.
  3. Add chili peppers and cook for another minute.
  4. Add the cooked rice and stir to combine with the aromatics.
  5. Add both soy sauces and stir well to coat the rice.
  6. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  7. If using, add the cooked protein (chicken, shrimp, or tofu) and stir to heat through.
  8. Serve hot, garnished with fried eggs and sliced cucumbers if desired.
