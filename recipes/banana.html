banana

deskripsi

Banana bread is a moist, sweet, and delicious quick bread made with ripe bananas. It is a popular treat that can be enjoyed for breakfast, as a snack, or even as a dessert. The natural sweetness of the bananas adds flavor and moisture to the bread, making it a favorite among many.

bahan-bahan

  • 1 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 3 ripe bananas, mashed
  • 1/2 cup sour cream or plain yogurt

cara membuat

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, and salt.
  3. In a separate large bowl, cream the butter and sugar until light and fluffy.
  4. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then add the vanilla extract.
  5. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients, alternating with the sour cream or yogurt.
  6. Fold in the mashed bananas.
  7. Pour the batter into a greased 9x5 inch loaf pan.
  8. Bake for 60-65 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
  9. Allow to cool in the pan for 10 minutes before removing and slicing.
