apple pie

deskripsi

These mini apple pies are so fun to make! You can use different types of apples and give some of them a lattice crust. They're delicious as-is or served warm with ice cream. Let everyone select their favorite! This recipe uses Granny Smith apples. I bought the mini pie dishes online.

bahan-bahan

  • 1 package refrigerated pie crusts
  • 2 Granny Smith apples, peeled, cored and diced
  • 1/4 cup white sugar
  • 1/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg
  • 1 tablespoon all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon butter, cut into small pieces
  • 1 egg, beaten

cara membuat

  1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees F (190 degrees C). Grease a mini muffin pan.
  2. Roll out the pie crusts and cut into small circles to fit the muffin pan. Press the circles into the muffin cups.
  3. In a medium bowl, combine diced apples, white sugar, brown sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg, and flour. Mix well.
  4. Spoon the apple mixture into the prepared pie crusts. Dot with butter.
  5. Cover with another circle of pie crust and seal the edges. Cut small slits in the top to allow steam to escape.
  6. Brush the tops with beaten egg.
  7. Bake in preheated oven for 20-25 minutes, or until golden brown.
  8. Allow to cool before serving. Enjoy!
